For every treatment page in `inc/data/treatments-seed.php`, the three standard tasks are: (1) a **professional, richer FAQ**, (2) a **lead-capture form** section before Related Treatments, (3) a **Before/After gallery** section before the FAQ. Step Book is NOT part of the three — only add it when the user explicitly asks.

The FAQ task means **rewriting the answers to be more professional and fuller** — not checking whether a FAQ "exists." A page already having 6 short one-line answers does NOT satisfy this. Use the tummy-tuck FAQ (slug `abdominoplasty-tummy-tuck`) as the quality bar: 2–4 sentence answers covering real patient concerns (procedure, anaesthesia, recovery & length of stay in Turkey, scars, permanence, naturalness, safety, how to choose a technique/type).

**Why:** The user said repeatedly that the point is the FAQ being professional, not merely present. Saying "FAQ was already complete" and skipping it is wrong and frustrates them.

**How to apply:** On each treatment page, actively upgrade the FAQ answers to the tummy-tuck standard as one of the three tasks — never report a FAQ as done just because questions exist.
