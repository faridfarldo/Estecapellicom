There are two main reasons why hair transplantation or other surgical procedures have historically been approached with caution in HIV-positive patients:

  1. HIV is an immune system disease, and because patients may carry an increased risk of infection, surgical operations could potentially endanger their health.
  2. HIV is transmitted through blood, and performing surgical procedures on HIV-positive individuals may pose a transmission risk for healthcare professionals.

Thanks to advanced techniques used in modern hair transplantation, the risk of infection has been significantly reduced. This is the primary factor that makes hair transplantation possible for HIV-positive patients today. When surgical protocols are carefully followed, the risk of transmission to healthcare professionals is substantially minimized.

In microsurgical procedures such as hair transplantation, the risk of infection is low. Moreover, regardless of HIV status, post-operative infection is undesirable for any patient, as it may result in graft loss. Therefore, success in hair transplantation for HIV-positive patients depends largely on strict infection control.

In hair transplant procedures specifically designed for HIV-positive individuals, an enhanced infection control protocol must be carefully implemented. The clinical environment should maintain hygiene standards above routine levels.

Before Hair Transplantation

The two most frequently asked questions are:

  1. I am HIV-positive. Can I undergo a hair transplant?
  2. Am I a suitable candidate?

If the immune system is strong enough, there is no medical barrier to undergoing hair transplantation. The patient's HIV specialist should be consulted before surgery. If necessary, immune-supportive treatment may be administered prior to the procedure.

The Hair Transplant Procedure

After determining candidacy, detailed planning is performed, evaluating both the recipient area and donor graft reserve.

  • Selection of technique
  • Hairline design
  • Local anesthesia
  • Graft extraction
  • Channel creation
  • Graft implantation

Hair transplant procedures typically last 8–10 hours. The operation is painless, though the duration may cause mild fatigue.

After Hair Transplantation

Patients receive detailed post-operative instructions. Following them strictly is particularly important for HIV-positive individuals.

  • Protect the scalp from sun exposure, dust, impact, friction, and water contact.
  • Sleep at a 45-degree angle.
  • Wear buttoned or zippered clothing.
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking.
  • Avoid intense physical activity for the first few days.

Recovery varies individually. In some HIV-positive patients, healing may progress more slowly. Medical follow-up is recommended if needed.
