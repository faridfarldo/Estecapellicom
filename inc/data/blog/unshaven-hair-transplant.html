What is a No-Shave Hair Transplant?

No-shave hair transplant, which can be performed using DHI or FUE hair transplant methods, is a procedure where the patient's head is not shaved. It is suitable for men and women with hair loss in a specific area who do not want to shave.

How is No-Shave Hair Transplant Performed?

The donor area is shaved as minimally as possible after identifying the most productive area to hide among existing hair. The follicles taken from here are directly implanted using the Choi Pen in DHI, whereas in FUE, the follicles are first collected and then individually transplanted into the opened channels.

After a no-shave hair transplant, patients can continue styling their hair as usual, and the donor area is hidden among existing hair, making it invisible.

Results of No-Shave Hair Transplant

Patients who undergo no-shave hair transplant experience less social discomfort during the healing period since the donor area is not prominently visible. Similar to other methods, the final results become fully apparent within 6 months to 1 year. The so-called "shock loss" period will also occur in no-shave hair transplant.

If the DHI method was used for the no-shave procedure, the micro-channels created by the automatic pen are much smaller, allowing the healing process to be faster compared to the FUE method.

No-Shave Hair Transplant in Turkey

In Turkey, no-shave hair transplant is frequently performed using the DHI method with Choi Pen. Mastery of the Choi Pen, combined with exceptional success and experience in hair transplantation, has made Turkey the world's number one center not only for hair transplantation but also for the more intricate no-shave hair transplant procedures.

Thanks to no-shave hair transplant, it is not necessary to shave the donor area and/or the entire head while regaining your hair in Turkey.

No-Shave Hair Transplant Costs

Compared to classic hair transplant, no-shave hair transplant costs are generally 25–33% higher. The procedure requires more attention, effort, and time, which increases the overall cost.

Who is a Suitable Candidate for No-Shave Hair Transplant?

Initially, no-shave hair transplant was primarily for those with hair loss in a specific area and for women. Today, it is chosen by a much wider range of patients.

With the diversification of the automatic pen used in DHI and the tips used in FUE, no-shave hair transplant has become suitable for more people. Specifically, it is preferred by:

  • Men and women who want a hair transplant but do not want to cut their hair.
  • Those who do not want others to notice or discuss their hair transplant in work or social life.
  • Anyone who wants a hair transplant without changing their appearance.
