Hair transplant surgery is an effective and permanent solution developed to combat hair loss, allowing us to achieve the most aesthetic and natural-looking results. The procedure involves transplanting the patient's own hair follicles into bald areas through a surgical operation performed under local anesthesia. Thanks to painless anesthesia techniques, hair transplantation in Turkey can now be performed with almost no discomfort, although individuals with a very low pain threshold may still experience some sensitivity.

Each graft is extracted individually, and hair strands are implanted one by one. It is a procedure that requires considerable precision, effort, and patience. The operation may sometimes last up to 8 hours; however, during the procedure, patients can watch movies, listen to music, or read a book.

Hair transplantation is classified as a minimally invasive cosmetic procedure. It is not as painful as commonly believed, and clinics provide various amenities to ensure that patients remain comfortable throughout the long surgical session.

Does Pain Threshold Vary from Person to Person in Hair Transplantation?

You will not feel pain during the hair transplant procedure because the scalp is numbed with local anesthesia. However, when the anesthesia injections are first administered, you may feel mild discomfort. For individuals with a very low pain threshold or needle phobia, tolerating this brief discomfort may be more challenging.

Many people who dream of restoring their lost hair postpone the procedure due to fear of pain during or after surgery. However, with the help of sedation and new needle-free anesthesia techniques, patients can now achieve their goals with painless hair transplantation in Turkey and confidently restore their hair.

Anesthesia in Hair Transplantation

Since the procedure is performed under anesthesia, no pain is felt during surgery. After the operation, painkillers are prescribed to manage potential discomfort, antibiotics are given to prevent infection, and additional medications may be provided according to the patient's needs to ensure a comfortable recovery.

As with any surgical intervention that affects tissue integrity, it is normal for the body to react, and mild soreness after a hair transplant is expected. Ultimately, hair transplantation is a surgical procedure; however, painless anesthesia techniques and methods involving micro-incisions such as Sapphire FUE and DHI have minimized discomfort to a very low level.

Pain After Hair Transplant Surgery

After the first night following the procedure, most patients experience significant relief. Provided they protect their head, they can resume most of their daily activities. Despite this general trend, it should be noted that experiencing some pain after a hair transplant is normal and expected.

Post-operative discomfort may last for several days. Even with painkillers, it may occasionally reach moderate levels. Symptoms such as numbness, itching, or tingling may also occur.

Although hair transplantation is generally considered a minimally painful process, it is normal to experience some discomfort in the donor area, the recipient area, and across the scalp after surgery.

Typically, pain completely subsides within 2 to 4 days, and within one week, most patients no longer have any complaints. At this stage, the recovery process continues, and the excitement of growing new hair begins.
