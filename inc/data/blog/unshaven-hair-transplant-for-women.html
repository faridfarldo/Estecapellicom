Hair loss in women manifests differently than in men, though it remains psychologically significant. Hair loss in women manifests as thinning hair due to aging, hair loss during menopause, and receding hairlines. Female pattern baldness, medically known as androgenetic alopecia, becomes more prevalent with age.

Hair Transplantation for Women

Historically, women avoided transplant procedures due to the requirement to shave the entire head. Modern techniques have changed this reality. Unshaven methods now allow women to undergo treatment while preserving existing hair length, making procedures considerably more appealing to female patients.

Unshaven Hair Transplantation

By unshaven hair transplantation, we refer to hair transplant surgeries performed using the DHI hair transplantation technique. DHI represents an advanced FUE variation where follicular units are individually extracted and transplanted using specialized tools without requiring advance channel creation.

DHI Unshaven Hair Transplantation

The procedure involves several stages: comprehensive health evaluation, detailed hair examination, graft number determination, personalized planning, anesthesia administration, and direct implantation using Choi Pen technology. Benefits include natural appearance, accelerated healing, reduced graft loss, and higher-density implantation capacity.

DHI Vita Unshaven Hair Transplantation

This proprietary Estecapelli technique enriches grafts with vitamins, minerals, and amino acids through cold vapor therapy before implantation, theoretically reducing graft loss and promoting stronger regrowth.

Hairline Lowering with Hair Transplantation in Women

Forehead aesthetics significantly influence female beauty perception. Procedures can advance the hairline approximately 1.5–2 centimeters while correcting irregularities.

How Successful Is Hair Transplantation in Women?

Hair transplantation is one of the most performed aesthetic procedures worldwide. Results in women are highly successful, similar to men. Success depends substantially on clinic selection and medical team expertise.
