Understanding HIV Status and Hair Transplantation

The medical term "HIV positive" refers to people who carry the virus but are not sick. Not all HIV-positive individuals are contagious, and being HIV positive is not the same as having AIDS. As emphasized in awareness campaigns years ago, excluding HIV carriers only leads to social stigmatization, and no one has the right to do so. However, healthcare institutions are required to take certain precautions according to patient rights and universal health rules.

Here, the safety of both the HIV-positive patient and other patients and the surgical team is ensured. The clinic has the right to refuse hair transplantation to HIV-positive patients if proper hygiene is not available or if there is a risk of transmitting the virus to other patients or the medical team.

Can HIV-positive patients get hair transplantation in Turkey?

Yes, they can. There are clinics in Turkey that perform hair transplantation for HIV-positive patients. However, not every HIV-positive patient is a suitable candidate, because the immune system of the patient must be compatible with the hair transplant procedure.

Precautions for hair transplantation in HIV-positive patients

Conscious patients who are aware of their HIV status usually know the medical procedures well. HIV-positive patients are typically very aware of how to protect themselves and others.

Rarely, some patients may discover their HIV-positive status only during pre-transplant tests. In such cases, the procedure may be postponed. There are three main points to consider:

  • Due to a weakened immune system, preparation may be required before any surgical procedure. The patient's immune system must be checked. In risky cases, the operation should be postponed.
  • HIV is transmitted through blood. Surgical procedures like hair transplantation must be performed under high hygiene conditions with proper precautions.
  • During and after the procedure, the HIV-positive patient must be maximally protected against infections.

Additional precautions for HIV-positive patients during hair transplantation include:

  • Paying strict attention to personal hygiene before and after surgery.
  • Avoiding anything that may weaken the immune system prior to surgery.
  • Informing the doctor of any discomfort before the procedure.
  • Isolating oneself before the surgery to prevent viral infections, if necessary.
  • During healing, temporary isolation may be a precaution.
  • Avoid alcohol, smoking, and other factors that weaken immunity.
  • Maintain proper sleep patterns.
  • Request information regarding the hygiene of surgical tools before and after surgery.
