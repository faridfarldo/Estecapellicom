Diabetes and Hair Loss

Diabetes may cause hair loss through circulatory problems, hormonal imbalances, and weakened immune function. Hair follicles may not receive adequate nourishment due to insufficient blood flow. Medications used in diabetes treatment can also trigger hair loss as a side effect. Once blood sugar stabilizes with proper treatment, hair transplants can permanently address lost hair.

Hair Transplant for Diabetic Patients in Turkey

Many patients with Type 2 diabetes successfully regain their hair through hair transplant surgery in Turkey. Pre-operative monitoring of insulin and blood glucose levels is essential, as the procedure lasts approximately 8 hours and requires stable blood sugar for proper healing.

Hair Transplant for Type 1 Diabetes

Type 1 diabetic patients generally cannot undergo hair transplantation because they cannot produce insulin naturally. The lengthy surgical procedure can cause dangerous blood sugar fluctuations, making the procedure generally not recommended for this population.

Hair Transplant for Type 2 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes is not considered a contraindication for hair transplantation in Turkey when blood glucose remains controlled. Medical teams may request consultation with the patient's diabetes physician.

Duration of Hair Transplant Surgery in Diabetic Patients

Minimally invasive techniques like DHI or Sapphire FUE should be prioritized to complete surgery efficiently while maintaining blood sugar balance.

Wound Healing in Diabetic Patients

Post-operative recovery differs significantly. Thousands of micro-wounds require longer healing periods in diabetic patients compared to non-diabetic individuals. Due to immune system-related complications, patients with Type 2 diabetes may be more prone to infection.
