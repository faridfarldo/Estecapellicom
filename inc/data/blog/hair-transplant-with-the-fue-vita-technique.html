What Is FUE Vita?

FUE Vita is an advanced version of the FUE hair transplant method, enhanced with vitamins, minerals, and amino acids. We anticipate that in the future it will be widely used in all FUE hair transplant procedures.

In the classical FUE technique, grafts collected from the donor area are separated into individual follicular units and kept waiting before implantation into the recipient area. During this period, there is a risk that hair follicles may lose viability. Weak grafts or those damaged during extraction are often lost at this stage or may shed after implantation. If the surgical team does not exercise sufficient care, such losses can ultimately result in unsuccessful hair transplant outcomes.

Why Choose the FUE Vita Technique?

After the discovery of the follicular unit as an independent anatomical structure, the FUE Hair Transplant Method created a revolutionary breakthrough in hair restoration. However, its main limitation is that extracted grafts must remain in isotonic solution for approximately 3–4 hours until the channel-opening phase in the recipient area. Since the first extracted graft waits the longest, it must be implanted first once the channels are created.

Factors such as the time between extraction and implantation, the temperature of the environment in which grafts are kept, the order of extraction, and the implantation sequence are critical elements determining the success of FUE hair transplantation. Experienced hands can achieve remarkable success even with a weak donor area, whereas insufficient experience may lead to disappointing results despite a strong donor supply.

What Are the Advantages of FUE Vita?

FUE Vita addresses this limitation of conventional FUE. The solution in which grafts are preserved contains vitamins, minerals, and amino acids that nourish the hair follicles.

Hair follicles nourished during the waiting period attach more strongly after implantation and gain strength more rapidly. This significantly minimizes potential graft loss in FUE procedures and enables stronger roots, leading to superior and more reliable results.
